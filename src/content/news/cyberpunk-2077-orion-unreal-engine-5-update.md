CD Projekt Red has formally announced that **Project Orion**, the codenamed full sequel to *Cyberpunk 2077*, has crossed its pre-production milestone and is now operating at peak production velocity out of the studio's newly forged North American headquarters in Boston, Massachusetts.

With veteran directors from *Phantom Liberty* helming the project, Orion represents the Polish studio's definitive migration from their proprietary REDengine to Epic Games' cutting-edge **Unreal Engine 5.5**.

## The Technological Shift: Why Unreal Engine 5.5?

While REDengine 4 delivered breathtaking ray-traced visuals and Path Tracing in Night City, the custom architecture created massive engineering friction during multi-platform ports. Transitioning to UE5 allows CDPR's world builders to leverage:

1. **Nanite Foliage & Geometry Streaming:** Eliminating all LOD popping artifacts even across towering mega-building skylines.
2. **Lumen Dynamic Global Illumination:** Enabling reactive neon signage that mirrors dynamic weather shifts, power blackouts, and police strobe reflections.
3. **Chaos Destruction Architecture:** Allowing dynamic environmental breach points during tactical combat encounters.

## A Grittier, Multi-Layered Urban Metropolis

Narrative Director Igor Sarzyński recently shared insights into the creative philosophy steering Orion:

> "Phantom Liberty proved to our team that grounding the story in emotional tension, moral ambiguity, and cinematic pacing resonates far deeper with players than superficial scale. In Orion, Night City is not merely wider—it is dizzyingly vertical and socially fractured."

Sources close to the development team hint that Orion will explore social strata never fully realized in the original game: from the subterranean sewer dwellings inhabited by rogue netrunners to orbital high-society shuttle stations towering above the smog ceiling.

Expect enhanced cyberware customization, deeper life-path branching that meaningfully alters faction loyalties throughout the campaign, and refined hand-to-hand combat mechanics inspired by martial arts action cinema.
