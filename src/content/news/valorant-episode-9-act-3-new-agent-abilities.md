Riot Games has officially unveiled the 27th tactical operator joining the VALORANT roster: **Verve**, an enigmatic electronic warfare specialist hailing from Seoul, South Korea. Designed from the ground up to counteract defensive stalling compositions (such as Cypher trapwires and Killjoy lockdown setups), Verve brings a high-tempo toolkit focused on electromagnetic denial and tactical reconnaissance.

## Verve Ability Breakdown

### [E] Signature Ability: Pulse Echo (Free recharge every 40s)
* Equip a compact sonic scanner. Fire to launch an oscillating micro-drone that sticks to planar surfaces.
* Upon impact, the drone transmits a 360-degree acoustic pulse that reveals enemies through walls who are currently sprinting, jumping, or firing weapons within a 15-meter sphere for 2.5 seconds.
* Enemy players shifting or walking emit no acoustic signature, creating immediate tactical counterplay.

### [Q] EMP Grenade: Circuit Snap (Cost: 250 Credits, 2 Charges)
* Throw a throwable node that activates upon ground contact.
* Emits a localized ionizing field that disables active Sentinel traps, camera feeds, and alarmbots for 6 seconds.
* Enemies caught in the detonation zone suffer a temporary 25% weapon reload speed penalty and scramble HUD radar mini-maps.

### [C] Kinetic Barrier: Static Rift (Cost: 200 Credits, 1 Charge)
* Deploy a horizontal energy field that absorbs incoming projectile flashbangs (such as Breach flashpoints or Skye birds).
* Does not block standard bullet trajectories, maintaining pure gunplay integrity.

### [X] Ultimate: Overdrive Matrix (7 Points)
* Verve activates an amplified cyberware rig that discharges an arena-wide magnetic surge.
* Suppresses enemy agent active abilities for 8 seconds within a massive cone and grants Verve +15% movement speed and instant weapon equip speeds.

Competitive players anticipate Verve will radically shift map dynamics on choke-heavy battlegrounds like Ascent and Bind. Verve goes live alongside the Act 3 competitive reset next Tuesday!
